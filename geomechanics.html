Fault Slip Assessment

  • Fault slip induced by pressure changes is a significant risk that can affect subsurface stability and project safety, making effective management essential.
  • Use Carbon Guardian’s geomechanical expertise to accurately assess the stress state and mechanical parameters of faults, providing crucial insights into fault behavior.
  • Employ the C44 platform to perform detailed probabilistic risk assessments focused on pressure-induced fault slip, allowing for a thorough analysis of potential scenarios.
