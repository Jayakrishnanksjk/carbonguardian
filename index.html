About Company

Operational Assurance
for Geological CO2 Sequestration

At Carbon Guardian, we are committed to advancing a Net-Zero future by reducing carbon footprints in the industrial sector through robust technological and regulatory frameworks. Prioritizing health, safety, and societal well-being, we support the transition with cutting-edge carbon sequestration technologies.

Our platform enables successful geological CO2 storage by specializing in reservoir characterization and surveillance, ensuring safe, efficient, and permanent storage in line with evolving regulations.

Value Proposition

Operational Assurance

Confidence

Public trust in CO2 storage depends on continuous, transparent monitoring. C44 provides independent measurement, tracking, and verification to ensure accountability and long-term confidence in storage projects.

Conformance

CO2 storage projects have different monitoring and reporting needs at each stage. C44 provides interactive dashboards, smart alerts, workflows, and detailed reports to help operators track performance and stay compliant with permits and regulations.

Containment

We know that storing CO2 underground has risks. C44 helps detect, reduce, and control these risks — including leaks, well failures, corrosion, and earthquakes caused by storage. Our solution uses advanced risk assessment methods to keep the storage system safe.
