About Company

Lifecycle Management of Geological Sequestration
and Underground CO2 Storage Sites

At Carbon Guardian, we are committed to advancing a Net-Zero future by reducing carbon footprints in the industrial sector through robust technological and regulatory frameworks. Prioritizing health, safety, and societal well-being, we support the transition with cutting-edge carbon sequestration technologies. Our platform enables successful geological CO2 storage by specializing in reservoir characterization and surveillance, ensuring safe, efficient, and permanent storage in line with evolving regulations.

Value Proposition

Operational Assurance

Confidence

To instill public confidence in underground storage of CO2, continuous and auditable surveillance is essential. C44 is an independent and trusted solution that provides reliable measurement, monitoring, and verification of CO2 storage over the long term. Our solution aligns with the project scope, ensuring transparency and accountability throughout the storage process.

Conformance

CO2 storage projects have varying surveillance and reporting requirements throughout their lifecycle. C44TM delivers actionable insights through interactive dashboards, smart alerts, integrated workflows, and comprehensive reporting. These tools enable project operators to ensure and demonstrate conformance of injection results with permits and regulatory requirements.

Containment

We understand the inherent risks associated with underground storage of CO2. C44 helps identify, mitigate, and contain such risks, including potential leakage from storage reservoirs, well integrity failure, corrosion, and induced seismicity. Our solution incorporates advanced risk assessment methodologies and techniques to maintain the integrity of the storage system.
